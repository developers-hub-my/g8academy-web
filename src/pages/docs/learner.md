# Learner guide

As a learner you watch a certificate's episodes, sit its assessment, and earn a signed credential that anyone can check without an account. This guide follows the order you will actually go through: look around, create an account, buy a certificate, learn, sit the assessment, then share what you earned.

## Before you start

- **Nothing is needed to look around.** The catalogue, the pathways and the first 2 episodes of every certificate are open without an account.
- **The academy must be selling.** If a series page says **This credential goes on sale soon**, the academy admin has not switched on payments yet. Nothing you can do changes that; check back later.
- **Your organisation may have given you a seat.** If your employer or university bought seats, they invite you by email address. Register with that same address and the seat (and the access that comes with it) is waiting for you. See [Organisation guide](/docs/organisation/).

### How the programme is ordered

There are three levels, and each one opens the next:

| Level | What it is | How it is assessed | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fundamentals** (2 certificates) | Where everyone starts | A timed quiz and a guided lab, graded by the platform | RM 149 each |
| **Practitioner** (4 tracks, you need one) | You can run it in production | A hands-on lab exam, marked by an assessor against a rubric | RM 249 each |
| **Architect** (2 certificates) | You can design it and lead a team through it | A capstone proposal, then a capstone project, then a panel defence | RM 399 each |

Prices exclude SST (8%). The rules the app enforces:

- You need **both** Fundamentals credentials before you can sit any Practitioner exam.
- You need **any one** Practitioner credential **and** an approved capstone proposal before you can sit an Architect capstone.
- You can watch and buy in any order. The order only applies to sitting the assessment.

## 1. Look around the catalogue

**Why:** every certificate tells you what you will learn, how it is assessed and what it costs before you pay.

1. Open the academy home page and click **Browse the catalogue** (or **Catalogue** in the top bar).
2. At the top you see the **Pathways**: each card lists the certificates in it, in order.
3. Below, filter by **Level** or **Track** on the left, or search by topic (for example "Kubernetes").
4. Click any certificate card to open its series page.

![The catalogue: pathway cards at the top, level and track filters on the left, and certificate cards](/docs/screens/catalogue.index.png)

**What you should see after:** the number of certificates that match your filters, each card showing its level, hours and episode count.

### Follow a pathway

Click a pathway card (for example **DevHub Certification Pathway**) to see its certificates in the order you take them, how each is assessed, and **What it costs** if you buy them one at a time. Finishing every certificate in a pathway earns a further pathway credential at no extra charge.

![A pathway page: the ordered list of certificates on the left, the pathway credential and the total cost on the right](/docs/screens/catalogue.pathway.png)

## 2. Watch the free preview

**Why:** you can judge a certificate before you create an account or pay.

1. On a series page, click **Watch episodes 1–2 free** (or **Start with #01, free** on a pathway page).
2. The player opens with a **Free preview** label. Episodes marked **Free** play; the rest show a lock.
3. Click **Next free episode** to go on. After the last free episode the player offers **Buy the credential**.

![The free preview player: a "Free preview" banner, the video, chapter markers, and an episode list where episode 3 is locked](/docs/screens/catalogue.preview.png)

**What you should see after:** the episode plays with chapter buttons under it. Keyboard: space plays or pauses, arrows seek 5 s, `<` and `>` change speed, `c` toggles captions.

## 3. Create your account

**Why:** an account keeps your progress, your purchases and your credentials.

1. Click **Start free** in the top bar (or **Buy the credential** on any series page, which brings you back to checkout afterwards).
2. If the academy has switched them on, you see **Continue with GitHub** and **Continue with Google** buttons above the form. Click one to sign up with that account and skip the rest.
3. Otherwise fill in the form:
   - **Full name**: exactly as it should appear on your credentials.
   - **Email address**: if your organisation invited you, use the address they invited.
   - **Password**: at least 12 characters, mixing upper and lower case, numbers and symbols.
   - **Confirm password**.
4. Click **Create account**.
5. Open the email we send you and click the link to confirm your address.

![The Create your account form: full name, email address, password and confirm password, with a Create account button](/docs/screens/register.png)

**What you should see after:** your dashboard. If you came from **Buy the credential**, you go straight to checkout instead.

## 4. Read the series page and check you can start

**Why:** the series page tells you, for your account, exactly what still stands between you and the assessment.

Every series page shows:

- **Requires / When to take it / Leads to**: where it sits in the programme.
- **What you'll learn** and **What you'll be able to do**: the learning outcomes. Each assessment question or rubric criterion maps to one of these.
- **What is assessed**: the project, the lab, the pass mark (70%), and the attempts included.
- **The credential you earn**: an example of what you will get.
- The price box on the right: **RM 149 + SST, one-off**, what is included, and **Buy the credential**.

![A series page seen by a visitor: level and track, requirements, learning outcomes, what is assessed, and the price box with Buy the credential](/docs/screens/catalogue.show.png)

Once you are signed in, the right-hand side changes to a checklist for your account, headed with the assessment type and how many steps are left (for example **Hands-on lab exam: 2 of 4 steps left**). Each line says **Done** or **Not yet**:

1. The prerequisites for the level (for a Practitioner: **Hold the DevOps Fundamentals credential** and **Hold the Software Engineering Fundamentals credential**).
2. **Watch 90% of the episodes**.
3. **Buy the credential**.

![A Practitioner series page for a signed-in learner: a watch-progress card and a checklist showing both Fundamentals done, watching and buying not yet done](/docs/screens/learner-series-signed-in.png)

**What you should see after:** you know which line to work on next. You can buy now and watch later; the assessment simply waits until every line is done.

> **Only want to watch?** A subscription (from RM 39 a month) unlocks the episodes of every certificate. It never includes assessment attempts. To sit an assessment you buy the credential for that series, even if you subscribe.

## 5. Buy the credential

**Why:** the credential purchase gives you every episode with no expiry, **2 assessment attempts**, and the signed credential when you pass.

1. On the series page click **Buy the credential**.
2. On **Checkout**, choose **FPX online banking** or **Debit or credit card**.
3. Read **Refund policy, in short** (see [Common questions](#common-questions)).
4. If you have a coupon, type it under **Coupon code** and click **Apply**. The total updates.
5. Check the **Order summary**: price, **SST 8%**, and the **Total**.
6. Click **Pay MYR …**. You are taken to the payment provider to finish; the academy never sees your card or bank login.

![Checkout: payment method choice, the refund policy, a Pay button, and the order summary with SST 8% and the total](/docs/screens/billing.checkout.png)

**What you should see after:** you come back to **Billing** with **You're in. Payment received.**, the amount, the method and the invoice number. Click **Start watching** to begin, or **View invoice**. A copy of the invoice is emailed to you.

![Billing after payment: "You're in. Payment received." with the amount paid, method, invoice number, Start watching and View invoice](/docs/screens/learner-payment-received.png)

If the payment did not go through, you are sent back to checkout with **Payment not completed. You have not been charged.** Try again with the same or a different method. If the bank is still confirming, Billing says so and your access switches on as soon as it is confirmed.

### Your invoice

Click **View invoice** (or any invoice number on **Billing**). The invoice shows the seller, you as the buyer, the item, SST and the total, and how it was paid. Click **Download PDF** to keep a copy, or **Print**.

![An invoice: seller details, bill to, the credential bought, subtotal, SST 8%, total paid, and payment details](/docs/screens/billing.invoice.png)

## 6. Find your way around your dashboard

**Why:** the dashboard is where you pick up where you left off.

After you sign in you land on **Dashboard**. It shows **Continue watching** (one card per series you have started, with progress toward the assessment and a **Resume** button) and **Credentials earned** with each credential's **Public verify link**.

![The learner dashboard: Continue watching cards for three series with progress bars, and two earned credentials with public verify links](/docs/screens/dashboard-learner.png)

For the full picture, click **My learning** in the sidebar. It shows:

- **Continue watching**: the last episode you played, with **Resume at …**.
- **Series in progress**: one row per series, with the watch bar (the tick marks the 90% gate), attempts left, and **Go to assessment** once it is unlocked. A series in cooldown shows **Cooldown** and **What to revise** instead. A practical you have handed in shows **Awaiting assessment**, or **Returned for changes** if the assessor sent it back.
- **Your path**: Fundamentals → Practitioner → Architect, with what you have earned and what each next step needs.
- **Pathways** and **Recent credentials**.

![My learning: continue watching, series in progress with assessment status, your path, and recent credentials](/docs/screens/learn.dashboard.png)

If your organisation put you in a cohort, My learning also shows **Assigned by** your organisation, the certificates to finish in order, and the **Finish by** date. Falling behind never locks anything; it only shows you the pace.

## 7. Watch the episodes

**Why:** the assessment unlocks only when every required episode is watched to at least 90%.

1. From **My learning** click **Resume**, or open a series and click **Start episode 1**.
2. Watch. The episode list on the right ticks each episode once you have watched 90% of it.
3. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move between episodes. If you come back later, the player resumes where you stopped.
4. Under the video you will find **Resources** (for example the series' source code repository) and **Discussion**.

![The player: video, episode title, Previous and Next, a My notes box, and on the right the series progress and the episode list with watched ticks](/docs/screens/learn.player.png)

**What you should see after:** **Series progress** at the top right reaches the tick and says **The assessment is unlocked.**, with a link to the assessment.

### Take notes while you watch

1. Under the video, type into **New note** and click **Add note**. The note is stamped with the moment in the video you wrote it.
2. Open **My notes** in the sidebar to see every note grouped by series and episode. Click a timestamp to jump back to that moment.

Notes are private to you.

![My notes with no notes yet, explaining that notes are stamped with the playhead, with Continue watching and Browse the catalogue buttons](/docs/screens/learn.notes.png)

## 8. Sit a quiz assessment (Fundamentals)

**Why:** passing the assessment is what earns the credential. Watching alone does not.

### Check the assessment page first

Open it from **Go to assessment** on My learning, or from the link in the player. The page shows the **Format**, number of **Questions**, **Time limit**, **Pass mark** and **Attempts left**, then **Can you start?**:

- **Prerequisites**
- **Every episode 90% watched**
- **An attempt is available**
- **No cooldown in effect**

Read **Before you start** at the bottom: the timer runs on the server and cannot be paused; the attempt works in one browser at a time; questions are drawn at random; unanswered questions are marked wrong; there is a 24 h cooldown after a failed attempt; and once an attempt starts the purchase can no longer be refunded.

![The assessment page: format, questions, time limit, pass mark, attempts left, the Can you start? checklist all ticked, and a Start attempt button](/docs/screens/assessment.intro.png)

### Start the attempt

1. When every line is ticked, the right-hand panel says **You can start now**. Set aside the full time limit somewhere quiet.
2. Click **Start attempt N**.
3. Read the confirmation: the clock starts now, it counts even if you close the tab, and the purchase can no longer be refunded.
4. Click **Start the clock** (or **Not yet** to back out).

![The Start attempt confirmation: the clock starts now and cannot be paused, with Not yet and Start the clock buttons](/docs/screens/learner-start-confirm.png)

The attempt is used up the moment you start it, not when you submit.

### Answer the questions

- The countdown is at the top right. The header also shows how many questions you have answered.
- Every answer saves as you go; you will see **Saved** with a time. If saving fails you see **Not saved; your answers are still in this window.**
- Click **Flag for review** on any question you want to come back to. The **Questions** grid on the right shows answered, not answered and flagged.
- When you are done, click **Submit**, check the summary, then **Submit answers**. Or **Keep working**.
- If the clock reaches 00:00, your saved answers are submitted for you.

![An attempt in progress: question 1 answered, the countdown and Saved time in the header, a Submit button, and the question grid on the right](/docs/screens/learner-attempt-in-progress.png)

**One browser at a time.** If you open the same attempt in another browser or device, the questions are hidden there and you see **This attempt is open in another window**. Your answers are safe in the window where you started, and the clock keeps running. Go back to that window to finish.

![This attempt is open in another window: the questions are hidden, the clock is still running, and a Close and go back button](/docs/screens/learner-attempt-other-browser.png)

### Read your result

After you submit you see **your result**:

- **Passed**: **You passed. Your credential is issued and verifiable.** The credential card on the right has **Open in your wallet**.
- **Not passed**: your score against the 70% pass mark, and **By learning outcome** showing which outcomes are **Below the mark · worth revising**. Correct answers are not shown, so the next attempt still tests what you know. The panel tells you when your next attempt opens (24 h later) and how many attempts are left.
- **Time ran out**: the attempt was submitted for you with whatever you had saved.

![A passed result: 100% against a 70% pass mark, a per-outcome breakdown, and the new credential with Open in your wallet](/docs/screens/assessment.result.png)

**What you should see after:** a passed attempt puts the credential in your **Credential wallet** straight away. If you have used every included attempt, the assessment page offers **Buy another attempt** (RM 49 for one extra attempt). The 24 h cooldown still applies.

## 9. Hand in a lab exam (Practitioner)

**Why:** Practitioner certificates are assessed on real work in a real environment, marked by a person against a published rubric.

1. Once the **Can you start?** checklist is complete, the assessment page says **You can hand in now**. Click **Go to the hand-in**.
2. Read the **Brief** and **How this is marked** (each rubric criterion with its weight, and the 70% pass mark).
3. Under **How are you handing it in?** choose:
   - **Repository URL**: public, or shared with the assessor. The default branch is marked as it is when you hand in.
   - **Zip archive**: for workplace code that cannot be shared as a repo. One .zip, up to 50 MB. Only the assessor marking it can download it.
4. Optionally add **Anything the assessor should know**: how to run it, what you did not finish.
5. Click **Hand in** and confirm. Handing in starts your attempt and uses one of them. You cannot change it afterwards unless the assessor returns it.

**What you should see after:** **Handed in. An assessor is marking it.**, with your repository, the time, and your note. If something needs fixing, the assessor returns it with notes on the same attempt; handing in again does not use another attempt. When it is marked, the result and the credential appear here and on My learning.

![A handed-in lab exam: the brief, "Handed in. An assessor is marking it." with the repository and note, and how this is marked](/docs/screens/assessment.practical.png)

## 10. Complete an Architect capstone

**Why:** an Architect credential means you designed a real system and defended it. It has three steps: **Proposal**, **Capstone**, **Panel defence**.

### Step 1: the proposal

You can write your proposal at any time, even before you hold a Practitioner credential. An assessor approves it before the capstone opens, so you do not build something that cannot be assessed.

1. Open the Architect assessment page. While prerequisites are missing it says **Meet the prerequisites first**. Click **Write your capstone proposal**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Working title**.
   - **The problem, scope and constraints**: who has the problem, what it costs them today, what is in and out, and the limits you must design within. Describe; do not paste anything confidential.
   - **Repository URL** (optional).
3. Click **Submit for approval**. You cannot change it while an assessor is reviewing it.

![The capstone page: three steps (Proposal, Capstone, Panel defence), the brief, the proposal form, and how the capstone is marked](/docs/screens/learner-capstone-proposal.png)

**What you should see after:** **Proposal sent. An assessor is reviewing it.** If the assessor asks for changes, you see **What the assessor asked for**; edit it and click **Resubmit for approval**. Once approved, the status reads **Proposal approved · capstone open**.

### Step 2: the capstone

When you hold a Practitioner credential, your proposal is approved, and you have bought the Architect credential and watched 90%, the capstone hand-in opens on the same page. Hand in the design document and the code in one repository or one archive (put the design doc at `docs/design.md` or as a PDF in the root), then click **Hand in the capstone**. It is marked against the rubric, criterion by criterion.

### Step 3: the panel defence

Once the capstone is marked, you are offered dates to defend your design before a panel. The credential is issued when the capstone meets the 70% pass mark **and** the panel defence is passed.

**Why:** your credentials are yours. Leaving an employer, or having a seat reclaimed, never takes one away.

1. Click **Credential wallet** in the sidebar. Each credential shows its status (**Verified**), level, issuer, issue date and ID.
2. Click a credential to open it.

## 12. Share your credential

**Why:** the verify link is the proof. Anyone can open it, without an account, and see the live status.

On a credential's page, under **Share it**:

- **Verify link**: click **Copy** and paste it into an email, CV or message.
- **Add to LinkedIn**: adds it under Licences & certifications, with the verify link as the credential URL.
- **Download certificate**: an A4 PDF with a QR code and the verify link.
- **Download Open Badge JSON**: the signed badge file, for other wallets and verifiers.

Click **See what employers see** to open the public verify page yourself. Below, **Assessment summary** shows how it was assessed, the pass mark, your score and what it attests.

![A credential page: the certificate, Share it with the verify link, Add to LinkedIn, Download certificate and Download Open Badge JSON, and the assessment summary](/docs/screens/wallet.show.png)

### What an employer sees

The verify page shows the status first (**Verified**, **Expired** or **Revoked**), then the holder, competency, level, issuer, issue date, series version and credential ID, how it was assessed, and how the signature was checked. They can download the PDF or the Open Badge JSON from there too.

![The public verify page: a large Verified banner, what this credential says, and how it was assessed](/docs/screens/verify.show.png)

Someone who only has the PDF or the badge file can go to **Verify a credential** in the top bar and paste the link or ID, upload the Open Badge JSON, or scan the QR on the certificate.

![Verify a credential: paste a link or ID, upload the badge file, or scan the certificate QR](/docs/screens/verify.index.png)

### Publish a public profile (optional)

Your profile is private by default. To list all your verified credentials on one page:

1. In **Credential wallet**, under **Public profile**, type an address in **Choose your address** (letters, numbers and hyphens).
2. Click **Publish my profile**. Your page is at `/u/your-address`.
3. To hide it again, click **Make it private again**.

Expired and revoked credentials are never listed.

![A public profile page: the learner's name and handle and their verified credentials](/docs/screens/learner-public-profile.png)

## 13. Billing and invoices

Click **Billing** in the sidebar. You see your subscription (if you have one) and every invoice, with its number, date, item, total and status, and a download button. Under each item the page tells you whether that order can still be refunded and why. Credit notes from refunds appear here as well.

![Billing: a list of paid invoices, each with a refund note and a download button](/docs/screens/billing.index.png)

## 14. Download or delete your data

**Why:** under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 you can take a copy of everything held about you, or have it erased.

Click **My data** in the sidebar.

- **Download my data**: one JSON file with your account, viewing progress, notes, discussion posts, orders and invoices, and every credential you hold, including the signed Open Badge files.
- **Delete my account**: type `DELETE` in the box, then click **Delete my account permanently**. This removes your account, progress, notes and discussion posts and signs you out. It cannot be undone, so download your data first if you want a copy.

What happens to the rest:

- **Credentials stay verifiable, with your name removed.** Each verify link keeps working and still says **Verified**, but the holder reads **Name removed at the holder's request**. They are not deleted or revoked, because employers may already hold the link.
- **Invoices and credit notes are kept** as tax records, no longer linked to an account.

![My data: Download a copy of your data, and Delete my account with an explanation of what verifiers see afterwards and a DELETE confirmation box](/docs/screens/privacy.data.png)

## Common questions

**Can I get a refund?**
Within 7 days of paying, as long as you have watched under 20% of a series and have not started an assessment attempt. Once any attempt has started there is no refund, because you have seen the questions. For a credential purchase the 20% is measured on the series you bought; for a subscription, on any series. **Billing** says for each order whether it is still refundable. Refunds are made by the academy to the original payment method, with a credit note; ask at the email address on your invoice.

**I subscribed. Why can't I sit the assessment?**
A subscription covers watching only. Buy the credential for that series to get 2 attempts.

**How many attempts do I get?**
A credential purchase includes 2. An extra attempt add-on (RM 49) gives 1 more. A seat from your organisation gives 3 for each certificate. After a failed attempt you wait 24 h before the next one.

**My computer crashed during an attempt. What now?**
The clock keeps running on the server and your saved answers are kept. Go back to the same browser if you can. If time runs out, the attempt is submitted with what you had saved. You cannot move a running attempt to a different browser.

**Do I lose my credentials if I leave my employer?**
No. A reclaimed seat removes the access that came with it, never a credential.

**Does a newer version of a series change my credential?**
No. Each credential is bound to the version you passed and keeps saying what that version assessed.
